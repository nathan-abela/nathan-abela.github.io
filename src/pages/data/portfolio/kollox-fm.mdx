Global radio directories barely cover Maltese stations - listings are incomplete, stream links are often broken, and station information is outdated. KolloxFM fixes that: a directory and web player for every Maltese radio station, with verified live streams in one modern interface.

Beyond listening, a dedicated Survey Results section covers every audience survey published by the Broadcasting Authority of Malta since 2015 - respondent counts, total radio listeners, and per-station audience shares - visualised with interactive charts and heat maps so a decade of national listening trends can be explored in one place.
